### Decisions & Outcomes
- **Flawed Strategy Diagnostic Protocol**: Rather than silently stalling or concealing heuristic weaknesses, flawed/naive educational algorithms (`Intersection.Smart`) carry explicit metadata and visual warning flare in the UI, informing users that greedy logic may prevent the board from being solved.
- **Top-Level Modular Test Architecture**: Standardized unit and integration testing under `tests/domain/` and `tests/strategies/` at the workspace root, eliminating nested `__tests__` folders and ensuring clean compiler and linter isolation.
